🕸️ Quick Hello Before You Start

Welcome to the Hollow! 🎃

Hey! 👋 Glad you found this page. Before you start scrolling through the guides, here's a quick rundown of what you should know. It's short, I promise.

The big rule — read this first ⚠️

You do NOT have to finish the Main Quest before doing a Side Quest. Both are open from the start. So if you want to chase the hidden badge first, go for it. Nobody's stopping you, and no rule is being broken.

How the nights work 🕯️

The Hollow opened on October 22. A new night unlocks every day after that at the same time each day. The finale lands on October 31. If a night button is glowing orange, its Side Quest and UGC are live right now. If it's not glowing, it's still locked — but you can peek at the guide ahead of time.

The claim deadline is the important part ⏰

You can keep claiming rewards until the deadline shown at the top of the nights box (all times are shown in your own local time zone). After that, the event hub closes for good. Earn the badge before then, and click the shimmering statue in the hub to actually claim the UGC. Skipping the statue is the #1 reason people lose their rewards — so please don't skip it.

Where these guides come from 📖

Everything here is real info from the community — players who actually ran the quests and shared what worked. If a guide says "not verified" or looks incomplete, that just means the steps haven't been confirmed yet. You can open ⚙️ Settings and run a Deep Search to have the page try to pull the latest details and add them for you. Deep Search results save on this device so they stick around.

One small favor 🤝

Please be cool. Don't spam the page, don't try to break anything, and don't stress if a step doesn't work on the first try. Games get updated, steps change, and we do our best to keep it fresh. If something feels off, it's probably just the game being updated.

That's everything. Scroll down, tick the box, and let's go grab those badges. 🎃
