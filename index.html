🕸️ Quick Hello Before You Start

Welcome to the Hollow! 🎃

Hey! 👋 Glad you found this page. Before you start scrolling through the guides, here's a quick rundown of what you should know. It's short, I promise.

The big rule — read this first ⚠️

You do NOT have to finish the Main Quest before doing a Side Quest. Both are open from the start. So if you want to chase the hidden badge first, go for it. Nobody's stopping you, and no rule is being broken.

How the night unlocks work 🕯️

A new night goes live every day at 2:00 PM PT. That's 5:00 PM ET, or 9:00 PM UTC. If a night button is glowing orange, its Side Quest and UGC are live right now. If it's not glowing, it's still locked — but you can always peek at the guide ahead of time so you're ready the second it drops.

Don't forget this part 👻

After you finish any quest, don't just close the game and move on. Head back to the main Hollow Hub and click the shimmering statue for that night. That's how you actually claim the reward. Skip it and you get nothing — so please don't skip it.

Where these guides come from 📖

Everything here is real info from the community — players who actually ran the quests and shared what worked. If a guide says "not verified" or looks incomplete, that just means the steps haven't been confirmed yet. You can open ⚙️ Settings and hit "Deep Search" to have the page try to find the latest leaked details and add them for you.

One small favor 🤝

Please be cool. Don't spam the page, don't try to break anything, and don't stress if a step doesn't work on the first try. Games get updated, steps change, and we do our best to keep it fresh. If something feels off, it's probably just the game being updated.

That's everything. Scroll down, tick the box, and let's go grab those badges. 🎃
